What is actually making the sound?

Sample playback and processing

The browser decodes an audio file, then plays it through a small Web Audio graph. The source recording supplies the detailed texture; code handles timing, pitch, filtering, and interaction.

Canvas is only the picture

This page uses Canvas for the waveform. Audio comes from the Web Audio API. Animation and sound are independent, but can share the same interaction timing.

Where models enter

A sound effect model can invent a texture or creature-like layer. Export a short clip, then trigger and shape it with Web Audio. A model is useful for raw material; responsive playback still happens locally.

Why browsers can feel silent

Browsers usually require a click or tap before sound plays. Sites also often avoid audio to prevent fatigue and surprise. Here, every sound follows an explicit action.
