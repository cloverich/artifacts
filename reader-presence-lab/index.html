Block-anchored coordinates vs. page fractions

On the landing page (/live.js), pointer coordinates are sent as fractions of the whole document (pageX / scrollWidth). On essays, coordinates are normalized inside the hovered [data-k] block so cursors land on the exact same word across wide and narrow screens.

Double-click anywhere to send a ping

Double-clicking inside a block broadcasts act.ping.send({k, fx, fy}), spawning a 12×12px ring that expands 5× over 1 second. Scroll this pane to watch your tick move along the 6px right-edge #heat strip.
