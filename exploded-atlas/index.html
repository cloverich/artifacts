Meaning belongs to the annotation.

The references combine a central material-rich model with a quiet editorial frame. Consistent projection and thin guides establish correspondence; numbers, short captions, and insets establish hierarchy.

Try reducing separation to see why labels need their own layout. Switch to the mesh to see the representation before styling. The pavilion cutaway removes front walls; the landscape profile clips the surface at a known model plane.

THE TWO EXPLOSIONS

Thematic / landscape

The same surface appears six times. Water and routes are authored overlays; vegetation is a synthetic pattern. These are different questions about one place, not six underground strata.

Assembly / pavilion

Groups have distinct elevations before separation. Moving them changes the presentation transform, not the stored model. The courtyard remains aligned across all levels.

A useful annotation budget

Names → one idea → source and method. Keep the inventory available through layer records. “More information” should mean more inspectable evidence, not smaller text.
