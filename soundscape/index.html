    Why these sounds?

    Small cues make focus and physical controls feel responsive. Longer cues distinguish progress, completion, and failure. Compare the same sampling workflow in Silent, Essential, and Full. Audio reinforces each visible result; the console is fully usable without it.

    One buffer, 28 slices

    Your mechanical / −8 semitone Foley exports are concatenated losslessly with 50 ms silent gaps. The supplied map differed from several WAV durations, so this map was rebuilt from sample counts. The whole sprite is embedded here and decoded once after you enable sound. Each cue reads an exact segment from the shared buffer; edges fade for 2–4 ms. Six voices maximum, per-cue cooldown, modest master gain, and a compressor control overlap. Stereo placement follows the control's horizontal location.

    The decode readout measures local decode work, not audible output latency. Base64 and sprites remain extractable. Packaging is a delivery choice, not license protection. Foley's official code is MIT; no separate paid-pack restrictions were found. Other commercial sound libraries need their own license review.
